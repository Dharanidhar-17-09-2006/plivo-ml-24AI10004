Strategic Decisions

Byte-level vs BPE: Hindi text requires 3 bytes per character. I initially moved to BPE to compress these sequences, but I realized that in a 2,000-step sprint, the model cannot effectively learn a larger embedding table. I decided to stick with the 256-byte vocabulary to ensure every parameter was updated frequently enough to converge.

Optimization: I identified that the constant learning rate in the starter script was inefficient. I implemented a cosine decay with a warmup to allow for a higher initial learning rate (6e-4) while ensuring the model settled into a stable local minima by the end of the run.

AI vs. Human Breakdown

Machine Done: I used AI assistants to generate the implementation for the AdamW optimizer and the boilerplate code for the Cosine Learning Rate scheduler.

Human Done: I performed all hyperparameter tuning and experimental design. I made the critical decision to revert from BPE back to the Byte tokenizer after analyzing the BPB regression, concluding that training efficiency per step was more important than sequence compression for this specific time limit.
